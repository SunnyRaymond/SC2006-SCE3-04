# NFR-09 first-use study

Status: **not conducted**. Automated tests and generated accounts are not participants.

Recruit exactly five first-time representative users of a Singapore EV charging planner. Record only anonymous participant labels P1–P5 and why each fits the intended audience. Obtain consent to observe and time the task. Do not record passwords, email addresses, precise personal journeys, or other unnecessary personal information.

Freeze the app version, browser/device, data mode, and task before the first participant. Use the same setup for all five. Each participant starts signed in as a Driver with the standard vehicle profile already saved (60 kWh, 17.5 kWh/100 km, Type 2 and Combo 2, AC 11 kW, DC 120 kW, reserve 20%). Account access and vehicle setup are UC-01/02 preconditions; this timer measures creating and comparing a charging plan.

Use the explicitly labelled Demo Fixture mode for a reproducible usability task. This study does not verify live provider behavior. Reset unsaved inputs between participants. The observer must not explain the controls or complete steps for the participant during the timed task.

Give each participant this task, without pointing at controls:

> Plan a charging stop for a journey from Nanyang Technological University to Marina Bay Sands. Your battery is at 18%. Depart in about 15 minutes, allow up to 45 minutes for charging and 15 minutes of extra driving, and keep the automatic charging target. Create the plan, then compare at least two charging options side by side.

Start the timer after the participant has read the task and is ready on the planner. Stop when a saved plan exists and the comparison view shows at least two options. Record the actual duration even if it exceeds three minutes. Assistance, inability to create/compare, or completion after 180 seconds counts as failure. Do not drop a failed participant or replace them with an extra successful participant.
