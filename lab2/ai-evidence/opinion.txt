PostgreSQL is a reasonable provisional choice for PlugPlan SG because related plans, immutable calculation records and moderation decisions benefit from relational constraints and atomic transactions. Its setup and administration are an added cost for a short course project, so SQLite remains a simpler option for a single-process demonstration until shared testing justifies PostgreSQL. We would reject the AI's blanket prohibition on deleting historical versions: immutability should prevent in-place edits, while deleting a saved plan must still make its data inaccessible through the application within 60 seconds under NFR-07.
